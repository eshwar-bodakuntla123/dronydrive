# Dronydrive unified design update

This version keeps the accepted homepage, scrollable experience, and the existing
working Free 360° viewer implementation.

The update focuses on visual consistency:
- one dark graphite/green palette
- Inter typography throughout
- consistent borders, radii and glass surfaces
- unified green CTA treatment
- consistent muted text hierarchy
- consistent hover/focus behavior
- Free 360° section visually belongs to the same product
- Free 360° viewer interaction itself is unchanged

The free viewer remains browser-only: selected images are not sent to the backend.
